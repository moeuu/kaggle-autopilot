This is ranked first because bounded held-out text optimization is directly aligned with the paired-lift objective. ([arXiv][3])

Full-body evidence is retained because description-only routing can omit decisive signal. ([arXiv][4])

SkillJuror makes this a necessary controlled ablation. ([arXiv][5])
