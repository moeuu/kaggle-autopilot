## Ranked shortlist

### 1. Cross-family GBDT core

**Leak-free features/encodings:** the 13 raw predictors; missing flags/counts and the sleep–stress–activity missingness regime; train-fitted logs, clipped ratios, threshold distances, ordinals, limited categorical crosses, frequencies, and quantile bins; five-way self-excluding multiclass target encoding only for XGBoost/HGBC/RealMLP views. **Models and key settings:** CatBoost GPU (`6000` iterations, learning rate `0.035`, depth `8`), native-categorical XGBoost (`3000`, `0.021296`, depth `4`), three OvR XGBoost members (`2500`, `0.04`, depth `6`), and regularized TE-HGBC (`600`, `0.035`, 31 leaves). **Runtime/memory:** 300–480 minutes, 3–9GB VRAM sequentially plus substantial host RAM. **Leakage risk:** medium unless every target/statistic transform is outer-fold fitted and inner-fold self-excluding. **Fallback:** identical CPU hist/CatBoost paths, then LightGBM on the same matrix. Competition evidence favors diverse probabilities over a single tuned tree. ([Kaggle][8])

### 2. RealMLP-TD-S diversifier

**Leak-free features/encodings:** outer-fold robust scaling and smooth clipping, explicit missing/unknown categorical codes, missing indicators, target-free engineered features, and only audited self-excluding target encodings. **Model and key settings:** packed pure-PyTorch RealMLP-TD-S-style network, ensemble size `8`, batch `512`, accumulation `4`, up to `20` epochs, patience `5`, learning rate `0.01`, weight decay `0.01`, class-weight exponent `0.75`, AMP FP16/auto. **Runtime/memory:** 240–360 minutes and 7–11GB VRAM. **Leakage risk:** low; practical risks are OOM, AMP instability, and partial OOF. **Fallback:** halve batch/double accumulation, use FP32, then restart all folds with the compact standalone topology. RealMLP’s paper and standalone implementation support this preprocessing and its value beside GBDTs. ([arXiv][2])

### 3. Guarded TabPFN-3 member, otherwise FT-Transformer/PLR

**Leak-free features/encodings:** raw/compact target-free predictors with outer-fold imputation, train-vocabulary category codes, and missing flags; no target encoding, ID, or test-derived statistic. **Model and key settings:** one local TabPFN-3 estimator, `fit_mode=low_memory`, `memory_saving_mode=true`, no resident KV cache on RTX3060, test chunks `2048`, deterministic seed `42`; require a verified local asset manifest and runtime preflight. If blocked or over budget, replace the entire member with three-fold FT-Transformer/PLR (`d_token=128`, two blocks, eight heads, batch `512`, up to `16` epochs). **Runtime/memory:** 240–480 minutes, 8–12GB VRAM plus CPU cache/offload. **Leakage risk:** low for OOF; licensing/provenance and ETA are the main risks. **Fallback:** full-fold FT, never a partial TabPFN/FT mixture. Kaggle lists a 0.95171 TabPFN-3 public score, while the official report covers this row/feature scale. ([Kaggle][3])

### 4. Nested calibration, blend, and pairwise boundary refiner

**Leak-free features/encodings:** exact full-coverage base OOF probabilities, centered log probabilities, margins, entropy, model disagreement, target-free raw features, and missingness regime; no public scores, hard submissions, test labels, or IDs. **Models and key settings:** five-fold cross-fitted identity/temperature/multinomial calibration; nonnegative arithmetic/geometric blends; multinomial logit stacker (`C=0.2`); three pairwise XGBoost refiners (`800` estimators, learning rate `0.03`, depth `4`) activated only on disagreement or margin ≤`0.12`. Promote only for ≥`0.00015` nested gain, ≥4/5 nonnegative meta-folds, and no class recall drop beyond `0.002`. **Runtime/memory:** 30–90 minutes, under 6GB. **Leakage risk:** medium-to-high unless every selection and refiner prediction is nested. **Fallback:** best complete single or uncalibrated nonnegative blend. Official guidance supports cross-fitted calibration and nested evaluation. ([Scikit-learn][4])
