## Ranked shortlist

### 1. Shared TDN–TSM reliability-gated multimodal model — recommended final candidate

**Leak-free features/encodings.** Build one outer-joined clip manifest keyed by `(action_id, subject_id, trial_id)` for train and `path` for test. Within every fold, fit visual normalization, numeric schema ordering, median/IQR scaling, Radar histogram edges, Skeleton scale fallbacks, parser-quality thresholds, class-sampling weights, and any probability calibration on fold-train only. Visual streams use per-clip fixed crops, normalized-time sampling, raw appearance, signed temporal differences, valid-frame masks, and crop-quality flags. IMU uses accelerometer/gyro/angle aliases, magnitudes, differences, device-location groups, masks, and parser confidence. Radar uses point coordinates and range/azimuth/Doppler histograms when aliases are recognized, otherwise fold-aligned robust numeric statistics. Skeleton uses root-relative, body-scale-normalized coordinates, confidence, recognized bones, velocity, acceleration, and valid-joint masks. Availability and reliability are explicit inputs.

**Models and concrete hyperparameters.** Scratch shared ResNet18-GroupNorm with three four-channel stems, TSM `shift_div=8`, and TDN-lite excitation at `12×160²`; one-layer visual BiGRU with hidden 128 per direction; IMU depthwise 1D CNN plus two Transformer layers at 160 steps and width 192; Radar 16×16 histogram CNN plus one-layer BiGRU at 32 frames/96 points; Skeleton joint MLP plus temporal depthwise CNN and two Transformer layers at 64 steps/width 192; six 256-dimensional modality slots; shared target-conditioned missing-slot generator; two-layer, four-head reliability-gated fusion Transformer. Train with AdamW, batch 2, accumulation 8, fp16, visual LR `2.5e-4`, sensor/fusion LR `5e-4`, weight decay 0.03, label smoothing 0.06, square-root class sampling, modality dropout 0.20, MixStyle 0.35, auxiliary CE 0.10, proxy alignment/task losses 0.03/0.02, supervised contrastive weight 0.03 after epoch 4, EMA 0.9997, and three synchronized temporal TTA views. TSM, temporal differences, transformer missing-modality fusion, and compact bottleneck slots are supported by primary research. ([arXiv][4])

**Runtime/memory.** Approximately 18–22 million deploy parameters, 72–90 MB FP32 after stripping optimizer, EMA duplicate, contrastive state, reconstruction heads, and auxiliary heads; target 9.5–11.8 GB VRAM using sequential visual streams, frame chunking, AMP, and gradient checkpointing. Estimated 18–23 hours on RTX3060 for one-fold promotion, three full subject folds, final refit, and inference. **Leakage risk:** high if users cross folds, statistics see validation/test, crop thresholds are tuned globally, the contrastive queue survives fold boundaries, or OOF teachers score samples they trained on. **Fallback:** quarantine only the failing parser/modality; batch `2→1`, accumulation `8→16`; frames `12→10`; image `160→144`; Radar points `96→64`; disable proxy/MixStyle/SupCon only after negative common-fold ablations.

### 2. Shared TSM visual–Skeleton challenger — strongest lower-complexity pipeline

**Leak-free features/encodings.** Use Depth_Color, IR, Thermal, numeric Skeleton poses, clip lengths, valid-frame/joint masks, modality availability, all-zero/unreadable flags, and crop/parser quality. Fit normalization, pose schema, robust scales, and body-size fallback on fold-train only. Temporal jitter is synchronized across modalities; spatial/style augmentation is clip-consistent.

**Models and concrete hyperparameters.** Shared scratch ResNet18-GroupNorm with per-modality four-channel stems and TSM at `16×176²`, one-layer visual BiGRU hidden 128 per direction, Skeleton temporal CNN plus two Transformer layers at width 192, and a two-layer 256-dimensional reliability-gated fusion Transformer. AdamW `3e-4`, batch 2, accumulation 8, maximum 24 epochs, label smoothing 0.06, square-root class sampling, modality dropout 0.15, MixStyle 0.35, supervised contrastive weight 0.03, EMA 0.9997, and three temporal TTA views. The official CUHK-X benchmark shows the strongest conventional single-modality performance for Thermal, Depth, IR, and Skeleton, while IMU and mmWave trail substantially, making this a principled challenger rather than a weak fallback. ([arXiv][3])

**Runtime/memory.** Approximately 14–17 million parameters, 56–70 MB FP32, 8–11.5 GB VRAM, roughly 3–5 hours for promotion or 10–13 hours for three folds. **Leakage risk:** room/camera/attire shortcuts, globally fitted crops or normalization, and accidentally reading Skeleton visualization images instead of numeric pose files. **Fallback:** use 160 pixels and 12 frames; preserve missing-Skeleton tokens instead of dropping clips; use Pillow/NumPy if OpenCV is unavailable; disable Skeleton only after parser shape and finite-value audits fail.

### 3. Scratch Depth+IR R(2+1)D–BiGRU — mandatory execution reference

**Leak-free features/encodings.** Sixteen normalized-time Depth_Color+IR frames at 128², one deterministic data-derived crop per modality, fold-train channel normalization, a temporal-difference channel, clip length, valid-frame mask, and missing-stream mask.

**Models and concrete hyperparameters.** Compact residual R(2+1)D CNN with channels `32-64-128-192`, one-layer BiGRU hidden 192, attentive pooling, and a 40-class head. AdamW `3e-4`, batch 4, accumulation 4, maximum 18 epochs, label smoothing 0.06, EMA 0.9997, and two temporal TTA views. This preserves the scored Kaggle reference notebook’s useful crop/cache/grouped-CV structure while rejecting its unresolved external detector and video pretraining.

**Runtime/memory.** Approximately 6–9 million parameters, under 36 MB FP32, 5–8 GB VRAM, and 2–4 hours for one real subject-held-out promotion fold. **Leakage risk:** low under subject-grouped folds, but path-derived test labels, sample-submission reuse, or accidental pretrained-weight loading are forbidden. **Fallback:** use this pipeline to validate archive discovery, target mapping, caching, fold integrity, OOF output, size accounting, and submission generation; never substitute constant or sample predictions.

## Promotion and deployment

Run all three candidates on the identical promotion fold. Promote the best real validation-accuracy family; optional complexity must improve by at least 0.002, and ties within 0.001 go to the smaller/faster model. Complete three-fold cohort-balanced subject-disjoint OOF only for the promoted family. Refit a single deploy checkpoint on all training users for the median best-fold epoch count, optionally using leak-free OOF self-distillation at weight 0.10 and temperature 2.0. Final inference uses within-checkpoint TTA, not a multi-checkpoint ensemble. The deploy state must remain below 95 MB in FP32 before any optional quantization.

Operationally, the official rule banning LLMs for Small Track development is a hard blocker: training and submission generation from these instructions require written organizer clearance, recorded by a local clearance file. Without it, the implementation may audit files and report the blocker but must not fit a model or emit a competition submission. ([OpenAIotLab][1])
