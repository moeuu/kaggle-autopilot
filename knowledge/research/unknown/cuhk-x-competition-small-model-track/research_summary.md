## Ranked shortlist

### 1. Shared-TSM proxy-slot multimodal model — recommended final candidate

**Leak-free features/encodings.** Build one outer-joined clip manifest keyed by `(action_id, subject_id, trial_id)` for train and by `path` for test. In every fold, fit visual channel statistics, robust numeric schema/median/IQR scaling, Skeleton body-scale fallback, class-sampling weights, and parser-reliability calibration on fold-train only, then apply the frozen transforms to validation and test. Visual inputs are synchronized normalized-time samples with three raw channels plus one luminance temporal-difference channel. IMU uses location-aware acceleration/gyro/angle channels, vector magnitudes, first differences, masks, and parser flags. Radar uses coordinates or range-angle conversion, Doppler/velocity, SNR/intensity, point/frame summaries, and masks. Skeleton uses root-relative, body-scale-normalized coordinates, confidence, bone vectors, velocity, acceleration, and valid-joint masks. Availability, valid length, and parser quality are explicit model inputs.

**Model + concrete hyperparameters.** Shared scratch ResNet18-GroupNorm with per-modality four-channel stems and TSM at `12×160²`; shared one-layer BiGRU with hidden size 192 and attention pooling; IMU depthwise 1D CNN plus two Transformer layers at 160 steps; Radar PointNet-style encoder plus BiGRU at 32 frames × 96 points; Skeleton joint MLP/temporal CNN plus two Transformer layers at 64 steps; shared target-conditioned proxy generator; six-slot, two-layer, 256-dimensional reliability-gated fusion Transformer. Train with AdamW, batch 2, accumulation 8, label smoothing 0.08, class-sampler exponent 0.5, modality dropout 0.20, MixStyle probability 0.50, auxiliary loss 0.15, proxy losses 0.05/0.03, supervised contrastive loss 0.05, EMA 0.9997, and three deterministic temporal TTA views. TSM, Transformer fusion under missing modalities, and proxy-slot completion are grounded in primary research. ([arXiv][3])

**Expected runtime/memory.** Approximately 18–20M trainable parameters, 72–82 MB FP32 after removing training-only heads, 9.5–11.5 GB VRAM with fp16 AMP/checkpointing, and 18–22 hours for promotion, three folds, final refit, and inference on RTX3060. **Leakage risk:** high if subjects cross folds, fold statistics see validation/test, the contrastive queue survives a fold boundary, or sample-submission predictions are reused; moderate otherwise. **Fallback:** quarantine only a failing modality, batch `2→1` with accumulation `8→16`, frames `12→10`, image `160→144`, Radar points `96→64`, disable proxy/SupCon only after negative same-fold ablations, then promote pipeline 2. Dependencies are standard PyTorch/torchvision, NumPy, pandas, scikit-learn, and OpenCV or Pillow.

### 2. Shared-TSM visual–Skeleton challenger — strongest lower-complexity pipeline

**Leak-free features/encodings.** Use Depth_Color, IR, Thermal, numeric Skeleton poses, clip lengths, valid-frame/joint masks, modality availability, and parser flags. Fit all normalization, numeric schema, and body-scale fallback values only on fold-train. Apply temporal jitter consistently across the three visual streams and Skeleton, and keep every spatial/style augmentation clip-consistent.

**Model + concrete hyperparameters.** Shared scratch ResNet18-GroupNorm + TSM at `16×176²`, shared BiGRU attention pooling, compact Skeleton temporal CNN + two-layer Transformer, and a two-layer 256-dimensional gated fusion Transformer. AdamW `3e-4`, batch 2, accumulation 8, up to 32 epochs, label smoothing 0.08, modality dropout 0.15, MixStyle 0.50, supervised contrastive weight 0.05, EMA 0.9997, and three temporal TTA views. The public CUHK-X benchmark lists stronger conventional HAR results for Thermal, Depth, IR, and Skeleton than for IMU or mmWave; these are method-selection evidence, not a competition CV estimate. ([OpenAIOT Lab][8])

**Expected runtime/memory.** Approximately 13–15M parameters, 52–62 MB FP32, 8–11 GB VRAM, 3–4 hours for one common promotion fold, or 9–11 hours for three folds. **Leakage risk:** room, clothing, camera, or subject shortcuts; accidentally loading Skeleton visualization images as numeric poses; fold-global normalization. **Fallback:** reduce to 160² and 12 frames; use Pillow when OpenCV is unavailable; use visual-only slots only if the numeric pose parser cannot pass audit checks.

### 3. Residual 3D-CNN + BiGRU — mandatory real-data reference

**Leak-free features/encodings.** Sixteen uniformly sampled Depth_Color frames at 112², fold-train intensity normalization, clip length, and valid-frame mask.

**Model + concrete hyperparameters.** Residual 3D CNN with channels `32-64-128`, two-layer BiGRU hidden size 192, AdamW `3e-4`, batch 4, accumulation 4, at most 20 epochs, label smoothing 0.08, and two deterministic temporal views.

**Expected runtime/memory.** Approximately 3M parameters, below 15 MB FP32, 5–8 GB VRAM, and 1.5–3 hours for one subject-held-out promotion fold. **Leakage risk:** low under grouped splitting, but the canonical review says the public notebook uses mock tensors and simulated metadata, so its behavior is not accuracy evidence.  **Fallback:** use it only to prove archive discovery, target mapping, grouped CV, OOF output, checkpoint sizing, and submission validation; never copy the sample submission or emit a constant class.

## Promotion, validation, and deployment rule

Run all three pipelines on the same first subject-held-out fold. Promote the highest real validation-accuracy pipeline, require at least 0.002 gain to justify optional complexity, and break ties within 0.001 by smaller serialized model and lower measured latency. Complete three-fold subject-disjoint OOF evaluation only for the promoted family. Refit one deployable checkpoint on all training subjects, optionally using fold-safe OOF distillation, then generate final test probabilities from that checkpoint with within-model TTA. Do not average fold checkpoints for the official submission unless the organizer explicitly clarifies aggregate ensemble-size accounting. No external data, external pretraining, path-string model, sample-copy route, or non-training submission is permitted.
